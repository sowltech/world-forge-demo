# Sample Delivery — Ash Empire

## Core Concept

A fallen solar kingdom is rebuilding after machine gods collapse the old economy.

## World Premise

Ash Empire follows a society trying to restore trade, legitimacy, and ritual order after the systems that powered it have failed.

## Factions

- The Ember Court
- The Guild of Salvagers
- The Silent Foundry

## Locations

- The Sunken Capital
- The Glass Dunes
- The Clockwork Harbor

## Character Archetypes

- Reluctant heir
- Mechanist archivist
- Rebel quartermaster
- Exiled priest

## Story Hooks

- Recover the last lawful currency
- Decide who inherits the broken throne
- Broker peace between salvagers and zealots

## Commercial Use Cases

- Novel series setting
- Roleplay server world bible
- Game campaign foundation
