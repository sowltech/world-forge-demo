# World Forge FAQ

## Is this AI-generated?

World Forge uses structured generation and human-directed worldbuilding. The public demo is browser-based and template-driven. Paid packs may include human refinement depending on the offer.

## What do I receive?

A structured World Bible that can support a story, game, roleplay server, comic, film concept, or creative IP.

## Can I use it commercially?

Yes, unless a custom agreement says otherwise. The buyer is responsible for ensuring their submitted idea does not violate someone else’s rights.

## Do you create full games or books?

Not in the Starter Pack. This creates the world foundation.

## Do you offer revisions?

Starter Pack includes one light revision pass unless changed by the final public offer terms.

## How long does delivery take?

Use operator-confirmed delivery timing only. If not confirmed, write: delivery timing confirmed after order.
