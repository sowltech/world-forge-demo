# World Forge Creative Pack

## Offer

A guided concept-to-bible package for creators who want a polished world brief and a reusable structure for future story, game, or brand development.

## Includes

- Concept intake and refinement
- World Bible draft
- One revision pass
- Delivery in Markdown and JSON

## Best For

- Writers
- Game designers
- Creative operators
- Small studios exploring a new world or setting

## Outcome

You leave with a structured world bible that is easier to review, expand, and hand off.
