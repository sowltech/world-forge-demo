# World Forge Demo Video Script

## Goal

Show how World Forge turns a raw concept into a structured World Bible in the browser.

## Length

60 to 90 seconds

## Script

**Opening**

World Forge turns a raw world idea into a structured World Bible.

**Problem**

When you only have a concept, it is hard to turn that idea into something organized, exportable, and ready to share.

**Demo**

Here is a simple example. Enter a world name and a concept, then generate the full World Bible.

The demo creates sections for the core concept, genre, tone, premise, factions, conflict, locations, hooks, commercial use cases, and next steps.

You can preview the result instantly in the browser, then download it as Markdown or JSON.

**Value**

That gives writers, game designers, and creative operators a structured starting point they can expand, review, and reuse.

**Close**

World Forge runs entirely in the browser. No backend. No accounts. No external AI calls.

Try the demo and turn one idea into a complete world bible.
